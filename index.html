How to Download Instagram Reels & Photos

To use our clean, ad-free utility, follow these three simple steps:

  • Copy the Link: Open Instagram and copy the URL of the public Reel, Photo, or Carousel you wish to save.
  • Paste the Asset: Navigate to the main interface at Social Save and paste the link into the dynamic input field.
  • Process and Save: Click the action button. The media will process near-instantly without any layout shifts or watermarks.
